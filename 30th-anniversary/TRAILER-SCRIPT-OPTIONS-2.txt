----------------------------------------------------------

born here, the one who followed a sound, the one who was invited. Then the relay runs back
the other way, youngest to oldest, each speaking about what 30 years means, until the
pioneer has the last word.

member, so RG is in full colour. The second half is warm colour throughout. Each person gets

Look and sound: it opens on black with a worship song heard faintly, as if through a wall,
and it gets clearer with each cut until it's full and present at the end. Cuts land on drum
hits. If worship-team footage is available, cut it between the interviews. (This needs a
worship recording or footage from the church.)

old house, Mafil born here, Ben following a sound here, RG welcomed here. Each "Dito" lands
as a hard cut, and the word DITO appears in gold, then translates to HERE.

Look: clean warm colour. Each "Dito" is a slow push-in. If footage of the church building or
empty sanctuary is available, it can go under the "Dito" lines.

Feel: rhythmic and poetic. Hard cuts on the chorus, softer cuts on the stories between.

Which to pick
-------------
- Passing the Light gives each person a moment and a name card. It's the clearest "this is
our church family" piece, and it can be built with what we have now.

- Follow the Sound is the most cinematic, but it needs worship audio or footage to really
work.

- Dito is the most poetic and memorable. The "DITO / HERE" word could also be used in other
anniversary material.
