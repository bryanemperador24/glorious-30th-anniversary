This script follows that sound back through 30 years: the instruments carried in the rain, the home God gave them, the moves of the Spirit in worship, the people in the ministries, the faithfulness that kept it going. It ends where it started, with Ben following the sound.
**Look and sound:** it opens on black with a worship song heard faintly, as if through a wall, and it gets clearer with each cut until it's full and present at the end. Cuts land on drum hits. If worship-team footage is available, cut it between the interviews. *(This needs a worship recording or footage from the church.)*
**Feel:** warm and rhythmic, music first.

The other four each tell what happened to them *here*: Sisa under an old house, Mafil born here, Ben following a sound here, RG welcomed here. Each "Dito" lands as a hard cut, and the word **DITO** appears in gold, then translates to **HERE**.
**Look:** clean warm colour. Each "Dito" is a slow push-in. If footage of the church building or empty sanctuary is available, it can go under the "Dito" lines.
**Feel:** rhythmic and poetic. Hard cuts on the chorus, softer cuts on the stories between.

### Which to pick
- **Passing the Light** gives each person a moment and a name card. It's the clearest "this is our church family" piece, and it can be built with what we have now.
- **Follow the Sound** is the most cinematic, but it needs worship audio or footage to really work.
- **Dito** is the most poetic and memorable. The "DITO / HERE" word could also be used in other anniversary material.
