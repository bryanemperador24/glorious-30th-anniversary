## Who said what

### Sisa: since 1998 (28 years). The pioneer's voice
- **Start:** "Nag-start ako sa Glorious Church taong 1998… doon pa sa silong ng lumang bahay." ("It started under an old house.") A friend, Vicky, brought her in.
- **Memory:** "Yung nag-trending." The time leaders suddenly left the church, which she called crippling and heartbreaking. *"Nasira niya, nawasak… pero nasaksihan ko rin kung paano ni-restore ng Diyos ang iglesia."* ("The enemy broke it, but I witnessed God restore this church.")
- **Faithfulness:** "From nothing into blessing. We had almost nothing to eat, not even fare money." Her life changed through prayer and the church.
- **30 years means:** "Tatlong dekada ng kabutihan ng Lord, ng katapatan ng Lord." ("Three decades of the Lord's goodness, the Lord's faithfulness.")
- **Next 30:** **"From glory to glory, onward to the next 30 years."**

### Pastor Ver: associate pastor, partnered with Bishop Jun
- **Start:** Says "1996," then "nung 2006 nag-meet kami ni Bishop Jun," who encouraged him to join the ministry. *(⚠ the year is unclear, so check it with him.)*
- **Memory:** **When God gave them the CAP building.** Before that they kept moving from place to place, *"rain or shine, nagbubuhat kami ng mga instrumento"* ("we carried the instruments"). Getting the building was the happiest day.
- **Faithfulness:** "Dito ako binago… pinagpala… pinaangat ng Panginoon." ("Here the Lord changed me, blessed me, lifted me up.")
- **Next 30:** **"Be strong and courageous."** Stand firm, don't give up, God won't leave us.
- **Bonus (invite):** "Walang mas mahalaga… kundi yung presence ng Lord. *My presence will go with you.*" "Inaanyayahan po namin kayo sa December 6 sa Country Club." This works well as a closing invite or promo.

### Mafil: born and raised in the church
- **Start:** "Born and raised na po ako… *the moment I opened my eyes*."
- **Memory:** Moves of God in worship, and especially **before Bishop passed away**: he wanted to be taken home while praising, and there were *"three consecutive Sundays"* of outpouring. Very emotional.
- **Faithfulness:** She grew in faith, embraced her calling, and saw her family serve God through the church.
- **30 years means:** "A big milestone… there were many times this church *hasn't been able to last*, but this church has **stood**, because it is really the church of God… **this 30th is proof of God's faithfulness**."
- **Next 30:** "An instrument not just for Baguio City but **all throughout the world**… more souls to be saved."

### Ben (Roland): 1–3 years. The newcomer who followed the sound
- **Start:** **"I first came to know Glorious Church when I heard the worship team while walking near the CAP building… I decided to go upstairs and follow that sound."** ← the best line in the set
- **Memory:** His first time on stage as a musician at Glorious.
- **Faithfulness:** He just graduated, passed the board exam, and is now a registered nurse. "God reminded me to be still and to be faithful."
- **30 years means:** "A powerful testament to God's enduring grace and miracles."
- **Next 30:** "I pray that Glorious Church continues to **inspire generations to come** and bring more people closer to God."

### RG (Argi): youth, joined about 2 years ago through an invite
- **Start:** Invited "last last year." The people who welcomed them led to a deeper relationship with God.
- **Memory:** Fellowship with the dance ministry. *"This church hindi lang po siya building, but it is a **family** in Christ."*
- **Faithfulness:** God gave them a community. "Kahit sobrang hirap ng buhay… through faith I can do anything with God."
- **30 years means:** "A **celebration of faithfulness**: our sacrifice, our prayers, and the lives God has touched."
- **Next 30:** "…share the word of God, share the love of Christ, and **share a light that shines so bright for all generations to see**."

## The story these interviews tell

**Theme: "30 Years of His Faithfulness". The church was moved, broken, and kept standing, and each generation found its way in.**

The best thing in this footage is that **Pastor Ver and Ben both talk about the CAP building**. One remembers when God *gave* it; the other *heard worship coming from it and followed the sound upstairs*. That gives you a 30-year bridge between the oldest and newest voices.
